Applied AI Firm — Brand Direction v0.1 · Internal
01 — The Position

Frontier AI,
measured in outcomes.

An applied AI firm for mid-to-large enterprise. We don't sell strategy decks or pilots that die in committee — we build AI solutions accountable to the number at the end. Capability, turned into realised return.

02 — What we stand for

We sit between a frontier that changes monthly and an organisation that moves yearly — and we turn the gap into operational value.

The operator stance. Most "AI consultancies" advise and disappear. We build, ship, and put our name on the outcome. The deliverable is value realised, not a recommendation.

That's rarer — and more defensible — in mid-large enterprise, where the room is full of talk and short on shipped results.

What we stand against. Hype-merchants. Endless pilots. "AI strategy" theatre. One-size SaaS. Fear-driven adoption.

The brand carries this as quiet confidence — restraint, not volume. The intelligence is in what we leave out.

03 — Name finalists

Two clean, ownable names — each with the meaning built into the etymology. Both survived live domain & trademark vetting.

● Vetted clear
Outturn.
/ˈaʊt.tɜːn/ · noun
“The quantity produced; the result.”

The anchor. A real but rare word that literally means outcome / yield. Grounded, operator-grade, anti-hype — it sounds like delivery, not promises.

Tone: calm, credible, built to age into a firm. The clock-hand mark reads as a measure turning outward into result.

● Vetted clear
Redix.
/ˈriː.dɪks/ · coined
From Latin reditus — “return.”

The frontier option. Coined from the root of return (as in return-on-investment). Sharp, technical, distinctly “AI-lab.” Closest in feel to your references.

Tone: modern, precise, scalable. The mark fuses an R with an upward return-arrow — ROI as a glyph.

04 — Typography

An editorial pairing: a sharp display serif for moments of authority, a clean grotesque for the work, a mono for the data.

Display / Serif
Frontier AI, measured in outcomes.
Text / Grotesque
We build, ship, and put our name on the result. Schibsted Grotesk · the workhorse for UI, body, and decks. Neutral, confident, highly legible at every size.
Data / Mono
ROI_REALISED  →  +34%  ·  CYCLE_TIME  →  −61%

Production note: these are free Google-Fonts stand-ins. The licensed targets are Söhne / Aeonik (grotesque) + a cut like Reckless or Editorial New (display serif) — the actual ElevenLabs / OpenAI register.

06 — Voice & lines

Understated. Specific. Never salesy. The confidence is in the restraint.

“Frontier AI, measured in outcomes.”
Primary line — states the position without overselling.
“From capability to return.”
Short form — for footers, signatures, decks.
“We put our name on the outcome.”
The operator stance — accountability as a promise.
“An applied AI firm.”
The flat descriptor — sometimes the most confident move is none.

Avoid: “AI that pays for itself” · “unlock the power of AI” · “revolutionise your business” — infomercial register, wrong for this brand.
